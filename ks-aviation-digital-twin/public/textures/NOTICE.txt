Optimized map derivatives (September 2026):
earth-2048.webp and earth-4096.webp are reduced-resolution versions of the existing earth.jpg in this project.
earth-turkey.webp is an exact equirectangular crop (20–49 E, 31–46 N) of that same image.
No coastlines or coordinates were redrawn. Blue-water presentation is applied by the renderer.
